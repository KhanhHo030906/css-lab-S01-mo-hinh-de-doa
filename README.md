# README - Lab S1, he thong ShopLab

## He thong
ShopLab la mot trang ban hang online gia dinh, gom ba thanh phan: trinh duyet
khach hang, may chu ung dung, va co so du lieu. Luong du lieu chinh la dang
nhap (kiem tra tai khoan qua co so du lieu) va dat hang (ghi don hang moi).

## Ba moi duoc chon xu ly
- M01: SQL injection qua ma khach hang khi truy van don hang (tac dong 5,
  kha nang 3, diem 15).
- M02: mot khach hang doc duoc don hang cua khach hang khac do thieu loc
  theo ma nguoi dung (tac dong 4, kha nang 3, diem 12).
- M04: do duoc mat khau do thieu gioi han so lan dang nhap sai (tac dong 3,
  kha nang 4, diem 12).

## Vi sao chon ba moi nay
M01, M02 va M04 nam trong nhom diem so cao nhat trong bang xep hang tam moi
de doa (theo tich tac dong x kha nang), va deu tac dong truc tiep toi du
lieu khach hang - tai san co gia tri cao nhat cua he thong ban hang. Ba moi
nay cung co chi phi xu ly tuong doi thap so voi muc rui ro giam duoc, nen
duoc uu tien truoc.

Nhung moi bi bo lai tam thoi la M07 (mat khau luu khong ma hoa, tac dong 5
nhung kha nang thap hon - 2, do can truy cap duoc co so du lieu truoc) va
M03 (chiem token qua HTTP, kha nang thap vi gia dinh moi truong noi bo da
dung TLS o tang ha tang). Day la danh doi co chu dich, khong phai bi bo sot.
